Revenue Model: No Middleman

Donations hit BGCA’s Stripe
directly. Period.

No DAF, no Endaoment, no Treasury float. PocketCache is a software vendor. BGCA is the charity. The money flows accordingly.

Each donor pays their round-ups plus a flat, mandatory $1.00/month app fee (only for months they’re actively rounding up). This is PocketCache’s only revenue. BGCA is never invoiced. $0, always. Separately, donors see a pre-checked toggle to cover BGCA’s card-processing costs (~2.2% + 30¢). This amount goes to BGCA as part of the donation. PocketCache receives none of it. Toggle on → BGCA nets 100% of round-ups. Toggle off → BGCA nets round-ups minus standard card processing (same as any card donation). Either way, BGCA pays PocketCache nothing.

Risk & Liability

What could go wrong?
Almost nothing lands on BGCA.

Money never touches PocketCache. BGCA owns its Stripe account and can disconnect us with one click. The regulated risk sits with Stripe and Plaid, by design.

Money Never Touches PocketCache
Donations flow from the donor’s card → BGCA’s own Stripe account → BGCA’s bank. PocketCache never sees, holds, or moves donor funds.
BGCA Owns Its Stripe Account
Stripe Connect Standard means BGCA has full control. You can disconnect PocketCache with one click, no lock-in, no permission required from us.
Who Owns What
Stripe: card data, payouts, dispute tooling. As merchant of record, BGCA handles disputes on these charges the same way as any card gift.

Plaid: secure bank/card connection; donor consents directly to Plaid.

SSO (Apple/Google): login identity; no passwords stored by PocketCache.

PocketCache: the round-up math and the app experience. That’s it.
Insurance Before Launch
E&O and cyber coverage will be in place before launch, alongside a contractual liability cap. The regulated risk (payments, bank data, identity) sits with licensed, insured vendors by design.
