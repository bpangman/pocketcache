How your giving works

Automatic, but never a surprise.

  • ✓ Purchases round up automatically Link a card, and everyday purchases round up to the next dollar in the background. No app to open, no extra step at checkout.
  • ✓ You always see the total before it charges The amount locks at month's end, you get an email with the exact total, and you have ten days to review, adjust, cap, or skip it before anything is charged.
  • ✓ Skip a month, no penalty Skip and nothing is collected on that charge date at all. Your $1 app fee for that month simply rolls to the next time you're charged, never waived, never doubled.
  • ✓ Cancel anytime Disconnect your card in one tap. No lock-in, no exit fee, no retention flow.

Where your dollar goes

Flat $1. Not a cut of every gift.

Many giving apps take a percentage of every dollar you give, quietly, forever. PocketCache charges one flat $1 a month, and only in months you actually round up. You can also opt to cover card processing, so 100% of your round-up reaches the cause.
