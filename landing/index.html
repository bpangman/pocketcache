The short version

Three steps. That's the whole product.

No new habits for donors, no new work for your nonprofit.

💳

Step 1

Link a card

Donors connect a card through Plaid, a read-only bank connection. PocketCache never sees or stores card numbers.

💰

Step 2

Purchases round up

Everyday purchases round up to the next dollar automatically. Spare change adds up in the background.

🏦

Step 3

One monthly charge

Once a month, the round-ups charge as a single amount that lands directly on your nonprofit's own Stripe account.
