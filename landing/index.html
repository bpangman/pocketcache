How it works

Set it up once.
Your donors give every day.

PocketCache is the software behind your program. You run it, you own it, and the money never passes through us.

📄

Step 01

Enter your EIN

We check your 501(c)(3) status against public IRS records, so only a real nonprofit can go live. No paperwork.

💳

Step 02

Connect your own Stripe

You are the merchant of record. Donations charge on your Stripe account and pay out to your bank. PocketCache never holds the money.

🔗

Step 03

Share your link, QR code, or widget

Post your link, print a QR code, or drop a one-line widget into your website. Donors do not have to download anything.

☕

Step 04

Donors round up automatically

Round-ups accrue all month. One monthly charge lands on your Stripe, and you issue the receipt, not PocketCache.

For donors

Spare change,
on purpose.

Round up what you already spend. You choose the nonprofit, you see the amount before it moves, and you can stop whenever you like.

  • 💰 A flat $1, only in months you round up The app fee is PocketCache's only revenue. It is flat, it is itemized on your charge, and it is not a subscription. A month your card never rounds up carries no fee. And if you skip a month, you are only charged in the months you give: nothing at all is collected on that charge date, and those round-ups are never collected later. The $1 app fee is not waived, though. It rolls forward and joins your next charge.
  • 🎯 100% of your round-ups can reach the cause A pre-checked option lets you cover the card-processing cost, so the nonprofit nets every cent of your round-ups. Uncheck it and it works like any other card donation.
  • 🧾 Your round-ups are tax-deductible The nonprofit is the merchant of record and issues your receipt. The $1 app fee is not tax-deductible.
  • 🔒 No passwords, read-only bank link Sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook, with an optional Face ID or Touch ID lock. Your bank connection is read-only through Plaid, and card data stays with Stripe.

How a month works

No surprises, and nothing is charged until you have seen the number.

  • ALL Round-ups add up Every purchase on your linked card rounds to the next dollar.
  • 1st The amount locks We email you the exact total for the month.
  • 10th Ten days to review Check it, adjust it once, cap it, or skip the month. Skipping collects nothing on the 11th and rolls the $1 fee to your next charge. The window closes on the 10th.
  • 11th One charge, from the nonprofit Round-ups, the optional processing cover, and the $1 app fee arrive as a single charge on the nonprofit's own Stripe account.

Months under the $5 minimum are not charged. That balance and the $1 app fee carry forward and settle within three months at most.
