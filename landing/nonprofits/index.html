Your donors

$1 a month, flat

Charged only for months a donor is actually rounding up, and itemized on their charge. It's PocketCache's only revenue, and it isn't a subscription.

Donors can also cover card processing, so you net 100%.

  • ✓A pre-checked option (opt out, not opt in) lets donors cover your card-processing cost, so your nonprofit nets 100% of round-ups when they leave it on.
  • ✓Charges only run once round-ups reach a $5 monthly minimum. Balances under it roll forward, and the $1 app fee rolls forward with them.
  • ✓A settle-up runs at least every 3 months, whether or not the minimum was reached.
  • ✓A donor can skip a month: nothing at all is collected on that charge date. The $1 app fee for a skipped month isn't waived, it rolls forward to the donor's next charge.
  • ✓Round-ups are tax-deductible and your nonprofit issues the receipt, since you're the merchant of record.
